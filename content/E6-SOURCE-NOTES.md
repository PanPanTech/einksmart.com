# E6 range revision, 2026-09-23

Public catalog: 13 diagonals, 14 configurations (13.3-inch CNC frame and insert are separate). References are the owner-supplied Canvas English specifications v1.0/v1.1 and September 23 range spreadsheets. Source files remain outside the repository; internal pricing and supplier terms are not published.

## Source precedence and unresolved points

- Product-specific specifications take precedence over the draft range table for finished-unit dimensions, software and delivery form. The table still has legacy statements about the 13.3-inch enclosure and pixel-density ranking; those statements are not copied.
- 1.54-inch resolution, outline, interface and pixel density are not confirmed. The catalog does not publish the tentative 240 x 240 value as a finalized specification.
- 7.09-inch units are DVT evaluation samples, not an unconditional mass-production offer.
- 7.3-inch panel resolution is documented, but enclosure, firmware and complete-unit details are not finalized. The page offers configuration review rather than asserting stock availability.
- 5.89-inch late-September sample scheduling is not a stock guarantee.
- A3 volume dates conflict between sources. The page requests current batch availability without asserting November production or present mass production.
- Calendar 7.5 uses a 7.5-inch E6 main screen and a 2.9-inch monochrome secondary screen. Its local Bluetooth app is not the proposed MagiRealm platform. Main-screen pixel density is approximate.
- No unconfirmed prices, whole-device zero-power claims, long battery-life guarantees, universal CMS promises or company certification claims are added.

The owner subsequently labeled sample photographs by diagonal. Ten product configurations now use these photographs, including the formerly diagram-only 31.5, A3 and 1.54 pages. `product-photo-sources.json` records all 33 source filenames, model assignments and source/output dimensions. The 7.3 page still uses a labeled format diagram. Unlabeled generic frame photos are not assigned to a specific SKU just to increase image counts. Photos of integration boards, optional outer frames and outdoor demonstration settings are captioned as references, not promises of included accessories or outdoor certification. Photographs do not change the documented supply or software status.
