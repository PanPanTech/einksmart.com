Before you inquire

Can I request one sample?

Request sample availability and a configuration-specific quotation. Lead time and certification documentation are confirmed before ordering.

Does the price include software and shipping?

The quotation separates hardware, optional software, customization and delivery. Sample pricing is confirmed for the selected configuration; freight, insurance and destination taxes are not implied.

Can it play video or rapid slideshows?

No. Color E-Ink is intended for still images and low-frequency changes. Ask to see a full refresh with your artwork before choosing it for time-sensitive signage.

Are scheduling and fleet management standard?

Not across every configuration. These functions require a confirmed software package and demonstration. Roadmap features are not included in the hardware promise.
