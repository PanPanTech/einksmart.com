Privacy & inquiry information

When you contact us

Guangzhou PanPanTech Co., Ltd. receives the contact details and project information you submit to respond, prepare quotations and follow up on your request. Visit and referral information

Session storage keeps the entry-page path, referring site and campaign tags while you browse. This helps us understand which content led to an inquiry. When Google Analytics is configured, we measure page visits, product selections and inquiry actions. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers or message text as analytics events.
