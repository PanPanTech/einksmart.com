E6 Panel Modules & TCON

A1, A2 and A3 Spectra 6 open-cell panels with matching TCON for frame makers and display integrators. Enclosure, host controller, power and software are separate.

Components for your own system.

The module quotation covers the open-cell panel and its matching TCON. A TCON is not a Wi-Fi player or a complete signage computer.

  • Panel + matching TCON; A3 uses an integrated TCON
  • No decorative frame, enclosure, battery or content platform
  • Samples, stock and production quantities confirmed by format
  • Integration drawings and interface documentation on request; some require NDA
